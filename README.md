# SmartRoute AI

SmartRoute AI is a cloud-based, autonomous customer-support and lead-routing platform using Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic AI workflows for B2B service teams.

## Overview

- **Intent Classification:** Automatically triages incoming messages into customer-support requests or sales lead inquiries.

- **RAG Knowledge Agent:** Answers routine support questions grounded in company FAQs and product documentation.

- **Dynamic Lead Qualification Agent:** Collects key lead details (budget, urgency, company size, and contact information).

- **CRM & Helpdesk Integrations:** Connects via REST APIs and webhooks to helpdesk platforms (e.g., Zendesk, Jira) and sales CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot).

- **Admin & Escalation Dashboard:** Enables support managers to monitor live chats, review classifications, and manually override or take over active conversations.

## Target Performance Benchmarks

- **Response Latency:** < 3.0 seconds average per interaction

- **Intent Classification Accuracy:** > 92%

- **Routing Turnaround:** < 30 seconds

- **System Uptime:** 99.5% across web endpoints during evaluation
